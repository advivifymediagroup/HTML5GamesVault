Trail Blaze

Steer the neon trail with your finger. Eat orbs, avoid everything else.

Steer the trail toward your finger. Avoid walls, mines and your own tail.

How to play

Your finger steers a glowing neon snake of light. Guide it to the golden orbs to grow longer and chain combos — but never touch the walls, your own trail, the spiky mines or the purple hunters that start chasing you later.

Controls

  • Mouse: move the pointer — the trail curves toward it
  • Touch: hold and drag your finger (keep it down!)
  • Orbs in quick succession build a score combo
  • The longer you get, the harder it is to avoid yourself
