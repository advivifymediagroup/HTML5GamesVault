Pick a grocery item, then place it in the correct aisle.

How to play

Sort the shopping list into the right aisle. Choose an item in the tray and then click the matching section for Produce, Dairy, Bakery, or Pantry.

Tips

  • Milk and yogurt are usually dairy.
  • Bread, buns, and pastries belong in bakery.
  • Beans, rice, and pasta are pantry goods.
