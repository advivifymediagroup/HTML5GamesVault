Tower Guard

Build towers beside the road and stop 15 waves of raiders.

Pick a tower, then click a tile beside the road.

How to play

Waves of raiders march along the road toward your keep. Spend gold on towers beside the road: cannons for steady damage, frost to slow, lasers to melt. Click a tower to upgrade it. Survive all fifteen waves.

Controls

  • 1 2 3 — pick cannon / frost / laser
  • Click a green tile to build, click a tower to upgrade
  • Space — send the next wave early
