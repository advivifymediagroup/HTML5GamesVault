How to play

Build words using the letter wheel. Every valid crossword answer fills its spaces on the board. Letters may be reused, and words can be entered in any order.

Power-ups

  • Hint reveals a random empty square.
  • Hammer reveals the square you choose on the board.
  • Find bonus dictionary words for extra points.

Controls

  • Tap letters or type on your keyboard.
  • Enter submits a word.
  • Backspace removes a letter.

Expeditions

Complete a crossword to add its landmark to your collection. Your progress stays in this browser.
