Shape Fit

Pack every piece into the board with no gaps left over.

Drag a piece from the tray onto the board.

How to play

The board was cut into the pieces sitting in the tray, so they all fit back in exactly. Drag each piece onto the grid; it drops in only where there is room. Click a placed piece to pull it back out.

Tips

  • Fill the corners and edges first
  • Click a placed piece to return it to the tray
  • Pieces keep their shape — they never rotate
