How to play

Notes stream down a neon highway in time with the music. Hit the matching key as each note crosses the glowing line — the closer to the beat, the more points. Keep the combo alive to light up the whole track.

Controls

  • D F J K — the four lanes
  • Touch: tap a lane, or use the buttons below
  • Turn your sound on — the music is part of the game
