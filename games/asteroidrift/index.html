Asteroid Drift

Use arrow keys or drag your ship. Survive the field.

Move left and right. The field accelerates the longer you survive.

How to play

Guide the ship through falling asteroids. There are no weapons here: clean movement is the whole game.

Controls

  • Arrow keys or A / D to steer
  • Drag on the playfield on touch devices
  • Three shields per flight
