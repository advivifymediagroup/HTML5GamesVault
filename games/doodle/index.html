Ready?

Auto-bounce. Move left/right onto platforms.

Arrows / WASD / mouse to move. Green platforms bounce.

How to play

Bounce from platform to platform and climb as high as you can. Green platforms are solid, blue ones slide, orange springs launch you further. Fall off the bottom and the run ends.

Controls

  • ← → or A D
  • Or use the mouse (target x follows cursor)
  • Walk off one side, come back on the other
