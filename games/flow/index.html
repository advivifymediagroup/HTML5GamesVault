Flow Connect

Join each pair of dots and cover every square.

Drag from one dot to its matching pair.

How to play

Drag from a coloured dot to the dot of the same colour to lay a pipe between them. Pipes cannot cross. To finish a level you must connect every pair and fill every square on the board.

Tips

  • Start in the corners — they have the fewest ways out
  • Dragging back along a pipe cuts it short
  • Every square must be covered, so the shortest route is often wrong
