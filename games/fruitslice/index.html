How to play

Fruit is tossed up from below. Drag across the screen to slice it before it falls back down — and keep well away from the bombs.

Rules

  • 🍎 Fruit = +1 (combo bonus for multiple in one swipe)
  • 💣 Bomb = 💥 lose a life
