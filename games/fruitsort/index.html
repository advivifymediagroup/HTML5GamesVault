Choose a fruit and place it in the right basket.

How to play

Group each fruit into the correct basket: Citrus, Berries, Tropical, or Stone fruits. Pick one fruit and click the matching basket.

Tips

  • Lemon, orange, and lime are citrus fruits.
  • Strawberry and blueberry are berries.
  • Pineapple and mango are tropical fruits.
