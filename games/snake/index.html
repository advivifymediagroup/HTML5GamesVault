Snake 3D

Guide the snake, eat apples, don't crash.

Arrow keys / WASD / swipe to steer.

How to play

Steer the snake around the board and eat the apples. Every apple makes it longer and a little faster. Run into a wall or into yourself and the run ends.

Controls

  • ↑ ↓ ← → / WASD
  • Swipe or use the D-pad on touch
  • Eat glowing apples → grow + speed up
