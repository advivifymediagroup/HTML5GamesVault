Store Sort

Drag or tap items into their matching shelf.

★★★
Tap an item, then tap its shelf. Dragging works too.

How to play

Move each product into the shelf with the matching label. Later levels add more aisles, tighter move limits, and similar-looking goods.

Tips

  • Use the shelf glow to confirm your target
  • Incorrect drops cost a move but never ruin the level
  • Finish with fewer moves for more stars
