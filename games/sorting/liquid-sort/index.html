Color Liquid Sort

Select a tube, then select where to pour.

★★★
Click or tap a tube to lift its top color.
Patterns and numbers are drawn on liquid layers so color is not the only cue.

How to play

Only the top connected layer pours. You may pour into an empty tube or onto the same color, as long as the destination has space.

Tips

  • Keep an empty tube as working room
  • Undo is unlimited
  • Later levels add more colors and taller planning chains
