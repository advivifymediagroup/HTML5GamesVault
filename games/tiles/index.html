How to play

Every tile is built from three layers stacked on top of each other: a background, a pattern and a shape. Click one tile, then click another that shares any one of those layers. The layer they have in common disappears from both. Strip all three layers off a tile and it leaves the board. Keep the run going without a miss to build your chain.

Tips

  • Any single shared layer is enough for a match
  • A miss ends the chain but costs nothing else
  • Tiles down to one layer are the awkward ones - clear them early
