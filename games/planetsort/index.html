Pick a planet, then place it into the correct orbit slot.

How to play

Sort the planets from the closest to the sun to the farthest away. Select a planet card from the tray, then place it in the matching orbital slot.

Tips

  • Mercury is the closest planet and Neptune is the farthest.
  • Use the order of the slots as your guide.
  • Place each planet once; each wrong move costs momentum.
