How to play

Group the large water bodies by ocean region: Pacific, Atlantic, Indian, and Arctic. Click a name, then place it into the proper ocean bucket.

Tips

  • Pacific is the largest ocean.
  • Atlantic lies between the Americas and Europe/Africa.
  • Arctic is the smallest and northernmost ocean.
