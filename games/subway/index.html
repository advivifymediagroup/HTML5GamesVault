Ready to Run?

Left/Right to switch lanes. Up to jump. Down to slide.

Arrows / WASD. Space also jumps.

How to play

The track rushes toward you — switch lanes, jump, and slide to get past whatever is in the way. It speeds up the longer you survive.

Controls

  • ← → / A D — switch lanes
  • ↑ / W / Space — jump
  • ↓ / S — slide under
  • D-pad for touch
