Push boxes onto targets. Arrows / WASD to move.

How to play

Push boxes onto the targets. You can only push, never pull, and a box only moves if the space behind it is clear.

Controls

  • ↑ ↓ ← → or W A S D
  • D-pad on touch screens
  • U Undo · R Reset

Tips

Once a box is pushed into a corner that isn't a target, it's stuck. Use Undo liberally.
