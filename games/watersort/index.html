Water Sort

Pour the colours between tubes until each tube holds just one.

Click a tube to lift its top colour, then click where to pour it.

How to play

Click a tube to pick up the colour on top, then click another tube to pour it. You can only pour onto an empty tube or onto the same colour, and only if there is room. Sort every colour into its own tube to clear the level.

Tips

  • Keep at least one tube empty as working space
  • Pouring moves the whole run of matching colour at once
  • Undo is unlimited — experiment freely
