Aim Trainer

Hit as many targets as you can in 30 seconds.

Click the targets. Misses cost accuracy.

How to play

Targets appear for a short time. Click them before they disappear. Smaller targets are worth more, and consecutive hits build a streak bonus.

Scoring

  • Every hit scores, small targets score more
  • Streaks add a bonus per hit
  • A miss or a timeout resets the streak
