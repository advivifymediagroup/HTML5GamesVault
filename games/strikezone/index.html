Strike Zone

Three taps per throw: position, aim, power. Ten frames — good luck!

Tap to lock your position.

How to play

A full ten-frame game with proper scoring — strikes, spares and a bonus tenth frame. Each throw takes three taps: lock the ball position, lock the aim, then lock the power.

Controls

  • Space / click / tap — lock position, then aim, then power
  • Aim for the pocket between the head pin and the 2 or 3 pin
