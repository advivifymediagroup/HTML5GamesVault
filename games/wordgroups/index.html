Word Groups

Find the four groups of four that belong together.

Pick four words that share something, then submit.

How to play

Sixteen words hide four groups of four. Work out what links each group, select four words and submit. Every group has exactly one correct home, so a word that seems to fit two groups is the puzzle fighting back. Four wrong guesses and the run ends.

Tips

  • Solve the group you are surest of first
  • If you are one word away you will be told so
  • Some words are planted to look like they fit elsewhere
