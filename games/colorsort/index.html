Colour Sort

Put the swatches back in order so the colours blend smoothly.

Drag a swatch into the gap where it belongs.

How to play

The board is one smooth blend of colour, but most of the swatches have been pulled out and jumbled into the tray. Put each one back where it belongs so the colour flows evenly between the fixed corner swatches.

Tips

  • The fixed swatches at the ends tell you where the blend starts and finishes
  • Work from an anchor outwards rather than guessing the middle
  • Drag a placed swatch back to the tray, or straight onto another slot
