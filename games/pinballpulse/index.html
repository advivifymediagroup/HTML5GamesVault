Pinball Pulse

Use left and right to work the flippers.

Hit the glowing bumpers, then keep the ball above the drain.

How to play

Pinball is a rhythm game. Keep the ball moving with the flippers and collect points from the three bumpers.

Controls

  • Left arrow / A: left flipper
  • Right arrow / D: right flipper
  • Tap the left or right side of the table
