Pinball Pulse

Hold Space (or Launch) to charge the plunger, then flip with ← and →.

Hold Space to charge the plunger, release to launch.

How to play

A full 3D pinball table. Hold Space (or the Launch button) to pull back the plunger, release to fire the ball up the lane, then work the flippers to keep it alive. Pop bumpers score points, and clearing all three drop targets raises your multiplier.

Controls

  • ← / A / Z — left flipper
  • → / D / M — right flipper
  • Space — hold to charge the plunger, release to launch
  • Touch: tap the left or right half of the table, hold Launch
