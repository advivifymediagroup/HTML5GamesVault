About

HTML5GamesVault is a collection of free games that run in your browser. There is nothing to download and no account to create.

What's here

Arcade games, puzzles, and a set of 3D titles. Everything runs on desktop, tablet, and phone, with keyboard, mouse, and touch controls where they make sense. High scores are saved in your own browser, not on a server.

Cost and ads

Every game is free to play. There are no paywalls or premium tiers.

Feedback

If a game is broken or unfair, it's worth reporting. New games are added regularly, and suggestions are welcome.
